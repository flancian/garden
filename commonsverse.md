# Commonsverse

- The interconnected ecosystem of diverse, autonomous [[Agoras]], digital gardens, and free knowledge repositories.
- Plural, resilient, and decentralized:
  - Rather than relying on a single centralized server, the client lens can query an array of Agora origins in parallel (`AGORAS = [window.location.origin, "https://anagora.org", ...]`).
  - Contributions are composited in real-time across peers, preserving local sovereignty and preventing single points of failure.
