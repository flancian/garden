It started with the trip to Ikea where we got a glass display case for a seemingly clear reason that diffused the moment we went to assemble it, considered the risks and implications and noticed upon even trying that we couldn't get all glass panes integrated without introducing some worrying tension into the assembly, like an armed bow waiting to shoot one day or night at random say at three in the morning, being of the disposition also of releasing all stored energy when the case inevitably falls or is knocked hard enough. 

These things having been considered we dutifully repacked it and brought it down to the nice Kellar that all Swiss apartments offer to their inhabitants, including renters like us, with the promise of returning it eventually giving way to the resignation that it would need to be given away or recycled privately, its heft requiring a van and serious intentions and interests to persuade at least two people to risk back pain and a possibly loaded bow.
