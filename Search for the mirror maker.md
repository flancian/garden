It started with the trip to Ikea where we got a glass display case for a seemingly clear reason that diffused the moment we went to assemble it, considered the risks and implications and noticed upon even trying that we couldn't get all glass panes integrated without introducing some worrying tension into the assembly, like an armed bow waiting to shoot one day or night at random say at three in the morning, or release all pent up energy when the case inevitable falls or is knocked hard enough. These things having been considered we dutifully repacked it and brought it down to the Kellar that all nice Swiss apartments offer to their inhabitants, with the promise of returning it giving way to a resignation that it should be recycled, its heft requiring a van and...
