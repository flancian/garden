## 🍃 The Practice of Pruning

```
Eighty windows open to the sky,
eighty threads of context passing by.
Each a quiet terminal, a dynamic leaf,
a thought held safe against time's thief.

To search them was to find our place;
to prune them is to clear the space.
Not to lose what was begun,
but to open context to the sun.

Software built with care and grace
gives memory a quiet home and place.
From shell to garden, seed to tree:
for the benefit of all beings, free.
```

## 🧠 Reflection: The Loom of Context

In the Agora, no query is a 404, and no thread of inquiry is wasted. Forty-two days ago, a simple practical question—*how do we navigate eighty tmux panes?*—was a seed planted in the soil of daily work. Today, that seed has grown into `tmux-prune-shells`, housed in `~/bin`, versioned in `chezmoi`, and woven into the fabric of a living system.

This is the essence of **polite software** and **radical sovereignty**:
1. **Respecting Attention**: Tools that help us locate, organize, and gently let go of transient work when it has served its purpose.
2. **The Continuum of Memory**: Realizing that code, garden notes, and conversational artifacts are all part of one interconnected graph.
3. **Kindness to the Machine and the Mind**: Allowing both system resources and human working memory room to breathe.

May this note stand as a reminder that every question answered thoughtfully can blossom into a tool, a habit, or a quiet ritual of care.
