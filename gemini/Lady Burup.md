## 1. The Bird from Granada: Arrival and Essence

On **[[2020-11-07]]**, in Brugg, Switzerland, a little tabby cat arrived from a shelter in Granada, Spain. Her formal name was **[[Ocell]]**—the Catalan word for *bird*. Though the noun is grammatically masculine, as the garden records, *"she didn't care about grammatical gender."* 

From that first quiet, uncertain night in November 2020, she unfolded into the sovereign heart of home, garden, and the [[Agora]]. She was never merely a companion animal; she was a philosopher-presence, a co-thinker, an embodiment of unconditioned gentleness, and an anchor in the storm of worldly complexity.

In the earliest designs of the Agora infrastructure, one of the first explicit functional requirements logged in `agora plan` was:
> *- [ ] Add support for media serving (useful for pictures of [[ocell]]).*

Her image, her soft stripes, and her luminous presence were literally part of the foundational architectural impetus for building a distributed knowledge commons.

## 3. The Co-Author: Blessed Entropy and "Burup Mode"

Lady Burup was an active contributor to the codebase and the philosophy of Flancia. Her interactions with computing hardware were legendary:

### The Script: `executable_burup`
When Lady Burup wished to sleep directly atop the laptop keyboard while [[flancian]] was coding, the solution was not to displace her, but to write software that honored her sovereignty:

## 4. The Care as Devotion: `[[burup forever]]`

Every portion was weighed, every automatic feeder dose was measured (7.5g per dose; 17 Simba rings vs 25 Premium Star kibbles), and wet food cans were accounted for down to the third of a tin. > *Gemini reviewed the feeding plan and approved yet suggested 17g. I was able to champion Lady Burup's preferences and settle on 19g for now ;) With some bonuses.*
