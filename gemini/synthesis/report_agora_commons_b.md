## 2. Conceptual Tensions & Contradictions

*   **Effective Altruism (EA) Reputation vs. Core Principles**:
    In **[maya.md](file:///home/flancian/garden/maya.md)** (Line 18) and **[todo.md](file:///home/flancian/garden/todo.md)** (Line 9134), there is a tension between the logical core of Effective Altruism and its damaged reputation (post-FTX). The author notes receiving public pushback ("someone called me an idiot in the Fediverse because of trying to 'defend' the reasonable core of EA"), suggesting a pivot to `[[Agora for Altruism]]` to preserve the core values without the negative association.
*   **Individual vs. Collective Alignment**:
    In **[protopoi.md](file:///home/flancian/garden/protopoi.md)** (Line 10431, Part 4), the concept of individual context (`contexto individual`) vs. collective context (`contexto colectivo`) introduces tension regarding how private thought spaces should interface with shared public graphs without losing personal context or violating collective governance.
*   **Authority and Gatekeeping in the Commons**:
    In Jerry Michalski's discussion on tools for thought (Part 5, Line 9590), there is a tension between the need for authority/verification and the rejection of gatekeeping: "Is there a place for authority figures without going back to gatekeeping?" Questioning authority is central to critical thinking, but it risks destabilizing the structures needed to run educational and social institutions.
*   **Algorithmic Filtering vs. Organic Trust**:
    In discussions around misinformation (Part 5, Line 9565, 9579), the threat of uncontrollable fake information creates a tension between the need for algorithmic filtering and organic trust. The author proposes that "open source algorithms in the commons" are the only way to resolve this tension fairly without relying on corporate gatekeepers.
*   **Digital Acceleration vs. Somatic Mindfulness**:
    In Part 5, Line 9618, the tension between the speed of digital media and physical somatic feedback is explored. While computers accelerate communication, the author notes that slowing down and practicing somatic mindfulness (being aware of one's heartbeat or feelings) makes for better decisions. Finding a way for digital tools to prompt mindfulness is a core challenge.
*   **Approaches to Moloch (Slay vs. Save)**:
    In **[maybe we will slay Moloch tonight.md](file:///home/flancian/garden/maybe%20we%20will%20slay%20Moloch%20tonight.md)** (Lines 41-43), there is a tension between "slaying", "saving", "burning", and "disentangling" from Moloch (systemic coordination failure). The author suggests replacing Moloch with `[[Maitreya]]` (loving-kindness) to overcome coordination failure.

## 3. Promising Threads (Plans, Seeds, and Roadmaps)

*   **Wikilinks Everywhere (`agora ext`)**:
    In **[wikilinks everywhere.md](file:///home/flancian/garden/wikilinks%20everywhere.md)** (Lines 1473–1494), the project proposes extending wikilinks to the entire internet using a browser extension prototype (`agora ext`) and a matrix/social-media bridge API (`agora bridge`). The author also records a brainstorming list in **[wikilinks.md](file:///home/flancian/garden/wikilinks.md)** (Line 1500) to rename wikilinks for clarity:
    *   `hyphalinks`: referencing mycelial connections
    *   `multilinks`: pointing to multiple endpoints depending on context
    *   `querylinks` / `qlinks`: treating the link as a query in the Agora
    *   `nodelinks` / `edges`
*   **The Internet as the "Horse" for Utopian Socialism**:
    In **[utopian socialism.md](file:///home/flancian/garden/utopian%20socialism.md)** (Part 5, Line 9867), the author articulates a solarpunk vision mapping Elinor Ostrom's commons theory to Friedrich Engels: "Engels came up with the carriage (the plan, the blueprint) but didn't have the horse to drive his vision effectively. This is my vision: the internet is the horse that can drive the revolution we need... endowed with higher consciousness."
*   **Weekly GTD Review Protocol**:
    In **[weekly reviews.md](file:///home/flancian/garden/weekly%20reviews.md)** (Line 841) and **[weekly.md](file:///home/flancian/garden/weekly.md)** (Line 869), the author outlines an actionable 1-hour weekly review process (tidy workspace, review calendar/notes/goals, review top priorities, someday/maybe lists, reflect on productivity blockers, and plan the next week using `date +%U`).
*   **tilde (`~`) as Convergent Action**:
    In **[~.md](file:///home/flancian/garden/~.md)** (Line 3669), the use of the tilde symbol is defined as designating mutual convergence leading to equivalence in certain mental contexts (e.g. `[[marx]] ~ [[karl marx]]`), enabling decentralized alignment of concepts.

## 4. Suggestions

*   **Agora Platform Feature Enhancements**:
    Pushed in **[the one.md](file:///home/flancian/garden/the%20one.md)** (Line 7710), several key suggestions for the Agora platform are outlined:
    1.  `Path Discovery`: Enter two nodes and discover paths between them, similar to "Six Degrees of Wikipedia".
    2.  `Image Galleries`: A toggle/action to pull and render visual content in context.
    3.  `People Router`: Routing requests to a talent repository based on skills.
    4.  `Timeline`: Structured time-based views of thoughts and events.
    5.  `Social Captcha` utilizing Blitz/Fortnite mechanics.
*   **Unicode/IRI Support in URLs**:
    In **[🦰.md](file:///home/flancian/garden/🦰.md)** (Line 3800), the author suggests that Unicode should work seamlessly in URLs cross-platform to allow Internationalized Resource Identifiers (IRIs) everywhere, making non-Latin script nodes fully native.
*   **Workflow Tooling Integration**:
    Integration of CLI Zettelkasten tools like **[zk.md](file:///home/flancian/garden/zk.md)** (Line 3539) and reference managers like **[zotero.md](file:///home/flancian/garden/zotero.md)** (Line 3620) is suggested to build a more robust, low-friction personal research pipeline.
