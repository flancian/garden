## ⚖️ Conceptual Tensions & Contradictions

1.  **Spiritual Wisdom vs. Historical Evil ([d t suzuki.md](file:///home/flancian/garden/d%20t%20suzuki.md) & [zen in the art of archery.md](file:///home/flancian/garden/zen%20in%20the%20art%20of%20archery.md))**
    *   *Tension:* D. T. Suzuki is highly respected for popularizing Zen Buddhism in the West and wrote the introduction to Herrigel's classic *Zen in the Art of Archery*. However, the garden notes that Suzuki was "perhaps a Nazi sympathizer" (d t suzuki.md: L1063), creating a profound contradiction between his universal, compassionate teachings and historical political choices.
2.  **Decentralized Anarchism vs. Global Liquid-Democratic Government ([an open letter to the agora.md](file:///home/flancian/garden/an%20open%20letter%20to%20the%20agora.md))**
    *   *Tension:* The author advocates using the Agora to "form a humanistic, rational world government based on direct liquid democracy complementing nation states" (L522). This proposal stands in contrast to the anarchist and communalist principles found in Bookchin (*The Next Revolution*, *The Future of the Revolution*) and Öcalan (*Democratic Confederalism*), which prioritize radical decentralization and the dissolution of top-down state power.
3.  **Collaborative Knowledge Commons vs. Solo Realities of Open Source ([working in public.md](file:///home/flancian/garden/working%20in%20public.md))**
    *   *Tension:* The Agora is built on the philosophy of a shared, collective "knowledge commons" with no intellectual property barriers. However, Nadia Eghbal's *Working in Public* highlights that 85% of successful open source projects are maintained by less than 5% of developers (L3521). This creates a functional tension between the ideal of egalitarian community collaboration and the physical reality of solo maintainers bearing the bulk of maintenance labor.
4.  **Aesthetics of "Good" Capitalism vs. Anti-capitalist/Cooperative Systems ([car analogies.md](file:///home/flancian/garden/car%20analogies.md))**
    *   *Tension:* In `car analogies.md`, the writer tries to conceptualize a "good" form of capitalism (investing to build/win together) compared to stripping for parts. Yet, the wider notes lean heavily into anti-capitalist, post-monetary solutions such as UBI modeled as a network flow problem, public utility commons, and platforms like the cooperative *Resonate* music stream.
5.  **Self-Observation vs. Radical Sovereignty ([tweets.md](file:///home/flancian/garden/tweets.md))**
    *   *Tension:* The author contemplates tracking mechanisms: "I'd like to [[keylog]] myself. Perhaps [[screenlog]], if that's a thing" (L3325). This desire for absolute self-data collection conflicts with the user sovereignty guidelines of Flancia and the acknowledged "risks of thinking in public" ([pasted image 20210621192811.md](file:///home/flancian/garden/pasted%20image%2020210621192811.md)).
